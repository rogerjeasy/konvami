Konvami brand guide

Konvami: Konversation + ami. Your conversation friend, in every language. This page holds the logo, colours, type and every exported asset for the iOS and Android apps, the stores and the web console.

The idea

The name

German Konversation + French ami (friend). Short, easy to say in English, German, French and Italian, and free on every check (stores, companies, trademarks, domains).

The face

A speech bubble that smiles back: your persona is a buddy, not a textbook. It listens, chats and laughs with you.

The tongue

In French (langue) and Italian (lingua), “tongue” and “language” are the same word. The playful tongue says: let's speak, and don't be afraid of mistakes.

Typography

Wordmark and headings: Lexend SemiBold

Hallo! Salut! Ciao!

The wordmark SVGs are converted to outlines, so they never depend on the font being installed. Lexend was designed to improve reading fluency, a good fit for a learning app. Licence: SIL Open Font License.

Interface text: Inter

Wie war dein Wochenende? Erzähl mal!

Excellent legibility at small sizes, with full support for German, French and Italian accents (ä ö ü ß é è à ç). Licence: SIL Open Font License.

Exported assets

Usage rules

Do

  • Keep clear space around the icon of at least ¼ of its height.
  • Use the white wordmark on dark or photo backgrounds, and the gradient or ink version on light ones.
  • Use the monochrome mark wherever the system recolours icons (Android themed icons, notifications).
  • Minimum sizes: icon 16 px, lockup 96 px wide.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or skew the logo.
  • Recolour the face or remove the tongue in full-colour versions.
  • Put the gradient icon on busy, similarly coloured backgrounds.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font. Use the SVG files.
