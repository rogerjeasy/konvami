| 2.3 | A "Confirm password" field is added | Requested on 2026-10-09 |

OpenAI consent is withdrawn by deleting the account, ElevenLabs by its switch |

## Visual language (v4)

- Warm paper (`bg` `#F7F6F2`) and ink (`#141513`) surfaces; in dark mode near-black paper and
  light ink. The main action is an ink pill.
- Colour blocks, each owned by one feature: talking is tomato, words are pink, the streak is
  butter, the level and the week's goal are forest with lime, the week's focus is ink
  (`components/tile.tsx`). No purple, indigo, lavender, gradients or glows.
- Screens the prototype does not draw take the colour of their feature's family, in a
  `PageHero` tile at the top (title, what it is for, the learner's own number in lime or ink):
  talking (role-plays, pen-pal) tomato; words (phrasebook, import, pronunciation) pink; level and
  goals (grammar map and topics, exam centre, a simulation's score, Then vs Now) forest; asking
  (the Tutor) ink. Profile's pages open the same way (`ProfilePage` `hero`): the learner's own
  things butter (personal, reminders, voice changes, help), goals and learning forest, partners
  tomato, memories and the read-aloud voice pink, the account and its data ink (security,
  privacy, plan, export, the owner's pages). Deleting the account and the legal texts stay on
  paper. A value picked from a short scale (days and minutes a week, the reminder's time, pen-pal
  notes and quiet hours) is a `Stepper`, not a wall of chips; a choice between named things
  (the exam to simulate) is a list of `OptionCard`s.
- Account and setup screens open the same way: signing in and creating an account tomato, the
  birthday and anything about the learner butter, email, passwords and AI consent ink, the
  setup's questions by subject (language and course pink, explanations ink, goals and level
  forest, partners and Persona Studio tomato). A sheet names its subject with an `IconBlob`
  beside its title.
- First-launch heroes sit on a block: the intro's slides tomato, butter and pink, the microphone
  tomato, the placement test and Face ID forest. Every call (practice, setup with Mia, the
  placement test, an exam simulation) is the forest block with lime (`BlockScope`).
- The learner's own lines are lime bubbles everywhere (the call, the transcript, the Tutor,
  pen-pal notes, Then vs Now); lines in the language learned are set in the serif italic.
- Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for titles and big numbers, Figtree for interface text, and the
  language learned always in Instrument Serif italic (`langBody`, `langLine`, `langLarge`,
  `langWord`, `langHero`).
- 56 pt pill buttons, 52 pt fields (radius 16), tiles radius 30, 44 pt minimum touch target.
- Learner mistakes use the amber "Let's polish this" chip, never red.

## Adapting the phone mockups to other sizes

The canvas draws a 390 × 844 phone. The app keeps that look on every window through
`HeroLayout` and `src/lib/layout.ts`: stacked on phones (hero ≈ 56% of a tall window), actions
pinned at the bottom, content scrolling on short windows or at large text sizes, and hero beside
text on landscape tablets and desktop.
