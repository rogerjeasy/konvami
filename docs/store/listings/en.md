## Description, App Store and Google Play (max 4000)

```text
Konvami is a language partner you talk to. Practise German out loud with AI conversation partners that listen, answer and help you say it better. Your partners are always AI and never pretend to be human.

SPEAK, DON'T JUST TAP
• Talk with a partner by voice whenever you have ten minutes.
• Choose who you talk to: a friend, a teacher or an exam coach. Give them a name, a look and a voice.
• Your partners remember what you tell them and ask how your job interview went.

GET CORRECTIONS THAT HELP
• Small correction cards appear while you talk, only when the app is sure.
• After each conversation you get a report: what went well, your mistakes, new words and next steps.
• Say a corrected sentence again and hear whether you got it right.

PRACTISE REAL LIFE
• Role-plays for situations you meet when you live in a German-speaking country, such as the bakery, the doctor or registering your address.
• Short guided lessons on one grammar topic.
• A phrasebook with phrases for a situation you describe.

REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
• Flashcards with spaced repetition. Answer out loud, or review your words inside a conversation.
• Ask the Tutor when a rule confuses you.
• A grammar map shows which topics you use well and which need work.

PREPARE FOR A SPEAKING EXAM
• Simulated speaking tests in the style of Goethe-Zertifikat A1 to B2, telc Deutsch B1 and the Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer (DTZ), scored afterwards as an estimate.
• Konvami is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Goethe-Institut, telc or the organisers of the DTZ.

BUILT AROUND YOU
• The app speaks English, Deutsch, Français and Italiano.
• A weekly plan, your progress over time, and reminders at the times you choose.

PRIVACY
• For people aged 16 and over.
• OpenAI processes your voice and text so your partner can listen and reply. You are asked before your first conversation.
• No ads and no tracking. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app.

Konvami uses AI. A partner can make mistakes, so check anything important.
```
