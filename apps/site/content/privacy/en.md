# Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{updated}}

Konvami is a language partner you talk to. To do that, it has to hear you, write down what you say and remember how you are getting on. This policy explains what Konvami keeps, why, who else receives it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It applies to the Konvami app on iOS, Android and the web.

Konvami is in its testing phase. Some features described in the app's design are not built yet; this policy describes only what the app does today.

## What Konvami keeps

**Your account.** Your email address, the first name your partners call you, your app language, your age group (we only check that you are 16 or over; your birthday never leaves your device), your answer to email tips, and your consent choices with the date you made them. Your password is handled by our sign-in service and stored only as a secure hash; nobody at Konvami can read it.

**Your learning profile.** The languages you speak and learn, your level, goals, exam, interests and how you like to be corrected, your placement test and its result, and a log of changes to your profile.

**Your partners.** The conversation partners you create or adjust: their names, looks, voices and roles.

**Your conversations.** The microphone is only on during a conversation, never in the background. For each conversation Konvami keeps:

- a transcript of what you and your partner said, and a second, word-for-word transcript of your own turns, so that the report can quote your actual mistakes;
- recordings of the turns, so you can listen again, as long as "Keep recordings of my conversations" is on (it is on unless you turn it off). When it is off, your voice is still used for the word-for-word transcript and deleted right after;
- the session report written after the conversation: corrections, words and next steps;
- for role-plays, which goals you reached.

**What your partners remember.** After a conversation, short notes about you ("Has a job interview on Friday") are written down so your partners can ask how it went. You can read, edit and delete every note, mark topics as off-limits, or switch memory off, in Profile › What my partners know. Konvami keeps at most 300 notes.

**Your learning tools.** The flashcards you save, your reviews of them, the questions you ask the Tutor with its answers, and the phrases you keep in your phrasebook.

**Your photo**, if you add one. It is private: only you see it.

**Usage.** How many voice minutes you used each week and an estimate of what each request to an AI provider cost, so that we can keep to the weekly allowance and the monthly budget.

**Reports you send.** When you report a line of a partner or a correction as wrong or inappropriate, we keep the report: your reason, your note and the text you reported, so that it can be reviewed. Only the person who runs Konvami reads it, without your name.

**Technical logs.** For each request to our servers: the time, the address requested, the result and how long it took. No IP addresses, passwords or contents are logged.

**Crash reports, usage counts and notifications.** If the app breaks, a crash report is sent to Sentry: the kind of error, where in the code it happened, and your device, system and app version. Anything you said or typed is removed from it before it is sent. Anonymous usage counts (the app was opened, a conversation started, feedback sent, each with the app version and platform and a random install number that is not tied to your account) are sent to PostHog (EU) only if you turn on "Share anonymous usage data" in Profile › Privacy and AI; it is off until you do, and turning it off deletes the install number. If you turn on reminders, your phone is registered for push notifications: Konvami keeps the notification token of your device.

**Purchases and feedback.** If you subscribe, the store handles the payment; Konvami keeps which plan you have and until when, and your account number is shared with RevenueCat so that a purchase reaches your account. If you send feedback in Profile › Help and feedback, we keep the category, your message and, only if you tick it, the app version and your device's system.

Please do not share information you would not want written down, for example about your health, beliefs or other people. If you do, it may appear in transcripts, reports and memories. You can delete a memory at any time; transcripts and reports are deleted with your account.

## Why, and on what legal basis

- **To provide Konvami** (your account, conversations, reports, cards, Tutor answers, memories, recordings): because you asked us to, under our terms of use (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To send your conversations to the AI providers named below**: on the basis of your consent on the screen "How Konvami uses AI" (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account; without it, the conversations cannot work.
- **To keep Konvami secure and affordable** (logs, sign-in protection, voice-minute allowance, cost limits): our legitimate interest in running a safe service within its budget (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).
- **To understand how the app is used and to find faults**: crash reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in a working app (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)); anonymous usage counts only on the basis of your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Profile › Privacy and AI.

Konvami does not use your voice to identify you. Your level, corrections and reports are worked out by AI; they help you learn and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you can change your level at any time.

Konvami shows no advertising, sells no data and does not track you across other apps or websites. It has no advertising tools, and its only analytics are the anonymous usage counts you can turn on.

## Who else receives your data

We use service providers who process data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement:

- **Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL** (Luxembourg) hosts Konvami in its Frankfurt, Germany region: our servers, database, file storage, the sign-in service, and the emails with your verification and password reset codes.
- **OpenAI Ireland Ltd** (Dublin) provides the AI. During a conversation it receives your voice and the transcript; to prepare your partner, it receives the parts of your learning profile and memories your partner needs; after the conversation, it receives the transcript to write the report, the word-for-word transcript and the memories, and it answers your Tutor questions. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes it.
- **Sentry** receives crash reports (see above), with everything you said or typed removed.
- **PostHog** (EU cloud) receives anonymous usage counts, but only if you turned them on.
- **Expo** (Expo's push notification service) receives your device's notification token and the text of a notification when Konvami sends you one: a written report, a scored exam simulation or a reminder after a quiet week.
- **RevenueCat** receives your account number and your purchases, to tell Konvami which plan you have. The App Store and Google Play process the payment themselves.
- **ElevenLabs** is optional and off until you turn it on in Profile › Read-aloud voice. If you do, it receives only the text it has to read aloud (a card, a weekly check-in), never your voice.

OpenAI processes data in the United States. The transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, with the amendments for Switzerland, in OpenAI's data processing agreement.

Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores and process your data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies.

We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

## How long data is kept

- Everything in your account is kept until you delete your account, except what you delete earlier yourself (a memory, a card, your photo, your recordings).
- Deleting your account removes your sign-in and everything stored for you at once, recordings and photo included. A note that the account was deleted is kept for one day, so that no late request can store anything again.
- Database backups are kept for 7 days and then overwritten, so deleted data disappears from them within 7 days.
- Technical logs are kept for 14 days.
- A photo you start to upload but never confirm is deleted after one day.
- OpenAI keeps what it receives for up to 30 days (see above).

## How your data is protected

Data travels encrypted (TLS) and is stored encrypted, in the EU. Recordings and photos are private: the app gets a short-lived link to play or show them to you, and to nobody else. Access to the systems is limited to the people who run Konvami.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **see** the data we keep about you and get a copy of it, in a common machine-readable format: in the app, Profile › Privacy and AI › Export my data;
- **correct** it: most of it can be changed in Profile;
- **delete** it: in the app, Profile › Privacy and AI › Delete account, or by writing to us;
- **restrict** or **object to** its use, and to **withdraw** your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before;
- **complain** to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, edoeb.admin.ch), in the EU the authority of the country where you live or work.

To use these rights, write to **{{controller.email}}** from your account's email address. We answer within one month.

## Age

Konvami is for people aged 16 and over. If you believe someone younger has an account, write to us and we will delete it.
